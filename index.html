ACT Overhead & Shoulder Routine

35–40 Min Standalone Conditioning for Steep Climbing

Rest Interval Timer
00:45
Warm-Up & Mobilisation (~7 Min)
Chest Opener 2 Sets × 60s Hold
Lie on a foam roller along your spine. Extend arms out to the side and let them hang to stretch the chest and front shoulder joint.
Focus: Thoracic spine extension and opening up overhead range.
1 2
Wall Slides (with ACT Band) 2 Sets × 10–12 Reps
Stand facing a wall, forearms on wall pushing outward against band. Slide up and down keeping hands, elbows, and shoulders aligned.
Focus: Depress shoulders down away from ears; keep lower traps engaged.
1 2
Block A: Primary Strength (~12 Min)
A1: ACT Pull-ups 3 Sets × 5–8 Reps
Loop band around forearms below elbows. Pull up while actively rotating elbows slightly inward under band tension.
Focus: Prevents outward "chicken-winging" elbow flare on hard pulls.
1 2 3
A2: Sword Pull 3 Sets × 8–10 Reps/side
Low band anchor. Pull diagonally upward leading with elbow, then extend forearm overhead in one fluid motion.
Focus: Dynamic rotator cuff stability along an overhead pulling line.
1 2 3
Block B: Overhead Control & Anti-Rotation (~12 Min)
B1: Upside-Down Kettlebells 3 Sets × 5 Reps (5s Hold)
Hold kettlebell bottom-up in a half-kneeling stance. Press overhead with a neutral, rigid wrist and hold.
Focus: Deep shoulder socket stability and wrist alignment.
1 2 3
B2: Power Plank 3 Sets × 8–10 Pulls/side
Forearm plank facing low band anchor. Pull band with one arm while keeping hips perfectly level to floor.
Focus: Links arm pull directly to core rigidity so feet don't blow off footholds.
1 2 3
Block C: Stabilization & Forearm Health (~7 Min)
C1: Rotation Curls 2 Sets × 8–10 Reps/arm
Extend arm upward/backward with max external rotation using light weight (<4kg), returning via curl motion.
Focus: Controlled rotator cuff movement through full overhead path.
1 2
C2: Core Pendulums 2 Sets × 8 Tilts/side
On back, legs at 90°, arms pointing up against band tension. Tilt legs side to side without losing upper tension.
Focus: Anti-rotational trunk stability when cutting feet.
1 2
C3: Finger Extensors with ACT Band 2 Sets × 15 Reps
Extend and spread fingers against resistance band placed across fingertips.
Focus: Balances forearm extensors and flexors to protect elbows.
